      About

      Every plate here is a real personalized-plate application that the California DMV flagged for review in 2015 and 2016. You read the request and the applicant's explanation, rule on it, then see what the DMV decided and what the reviewer wrote.

      Where the data comes from

      The records were released in response to a public records request and compiled by Noah Veltman in veltman/ca-license-plates. Plates, explanations and reviewer comments are quoted exactly as they appear, typos included.

      To keep this a game, rows with slurs or hate terms were left out, as were rows that seemed to name a private person or include contact details. Crude and sexual humor was kept. Plates were also picked so that half of each docket was approved and half denied; in the full records, about four in five were denied.

      How to play

      Tap Approve or Deny, swipe the plate right or left, or use the keyboard: D or → approves, A or ← denies, N moves on. The daily docket is the same ten plates for everyone and changes at midnight UTC. Streak mode keeps going until your first wrong call.
